FAQ

Answers to what patients ask most.

How much does treatment cost?+

Every mouth is different. After your free consultation you'll receive a written, itemized quote — with financing options if you need them.

How long does Invisalign treatment take?+

Most cases complete in 6 to 18 months, depending on complexity. We'll give you an exact timeline at your first visit.

Are dental implants painful?+

The procedure is done under local anesthetic and most patients report minimal discomfort. Sedation is available if you'd prefer.

Do you offer payment plans?+

Yes — 0% financing over 12 or 24 months is available on most treatments, subject to approval.

How do I book an appointment?+

Use the online form above or call us at (555) 123-4567. We confirm most requests within one business hour.
